❓ Troubleshooting Common Issues

🔧 "This app can't run on your PC"
You are likely running a 32-bit Windows system. This companion requires 64-bit Windows 10 or 11. Try on a different PC, or check your Windows version by right-clicking "This PC" > Properties.
🔧 Antivirus deleted the file
This is often a false positive. Add an exception in your antivirus settings for the downloaded folder, or temporarily disable it just during extraction and the first launch. Run it again from the official link.
🔧 Game still laggy after optimization
Check your laptop/PC cooling—overheating drops performance. Close other background apps, update your graphics drivers from the GPU manufacturer's website (NVIDIA, AMD, Intel), and lower resolution to 1080p.
🔧 Can't find my controller
Make sure the controller is plugged in (or Bluetooth paired) before opening the companion. Also, go to Windows Settings > Devices and check it's detected. Reinstall any driver software that came with the controller.
